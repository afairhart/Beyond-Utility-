Investor FAQ

Common questions from prospective limited partners.

What is the target fund size?
$12.5M USD.
How many limited partners are expected in this fund?
A total of 10–20 total partners is expected.
What is Alex's financial contribution to the fund?
Alex's contribution is 1% of the total fund value, which allows him to participate as a mid-career professional. This contribution is covered through both cash and fee waivers, and is contributed at each capital call period.
How does the fund structure protect LPs in distributions?
The fund uses a "European" distribution waterfall. This approach requires investors to receive their entire capital contribution and a preferred return across the entire fund before the fund manager receives any performance-based profit.
What are the operational, economic and governance terms of the fund?
These terms and many others are covered in our Limited Partner Agreement document, expertly crafted by our legal team at TIL Law. The LPA is available upon request to prospective investors.
